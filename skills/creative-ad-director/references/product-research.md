# Product URL research and visual recovery

Read this before using a product URL, search result, review, uploaded pack, or claim. The goal is a source-stamped evidence base, not a marketing rewrite of whatever the page says.

## Evidence hierarchy

Use the narrowest authoritative source available:

1. User-approved product brief, packaging/legal copy, and supplied media.
2. Official product detail page, manual, specification sheet, verified brand store, or platform product data.
3. Official support, FAQ, shipping, return, warranty, and promotion terms.
4. Identifiable customer reviews or creator demonstrations, labeled as anecdotal.
5. Search snippets, reseller copy, social posts, and competitor pages, used only as leads until verified.

For time-sensitive facts record market/currency and `captured_at`. Treat price, stock, coupon, delivery estimate, review count, rating, and promotion dates as volatile. Do not reuse them in an ad without checking the final landing page again.

## Product Evidence Sheet

Save a real readable canvas artifact with these fields:

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Source | Exact URL/file/asset and source type |
| Captured | Date/time and market/locale when visible |
| Product identity | Brand, product, model/SKU, variant |
| Audience/problem | Page-stated use case versus agent inference |
| Features | Concrete mechanism or attribute |
| Benefits | Supported user outcome linked to a feature |
| Proof | Demonstration, measurement, certification, warranty, or labeled anecdote |
| Offer | Price, discount, bundle, shipping, returns, expiry and conditions |
| Claims | Exact or tightly paraphrased wording plus evidence location |
| Visual references | Actual imported asset IDs or still-unverified media URLs |
| Unknowns/risks | Missing visual, contradictory fact, fine print, or unsupported claim |

Use three statuses: **Verified**, **Inference**, and **Unknown**. A page's own marketing statement can be verified as “the page states X” without proving X is objectively true. Do not upgrade testimonials, star ratings, badges, or comparative claims into scientific evidence.

## When built-in browsing cannot analyze webpage images

Text access, HTML access, a direct image URL, a thumbnail, and actual pixel inspection are different evidence states.

1. Extract accessible text, JSON-LD, Open Graph metadata, alt text, variant metadata, manuals, and direct media URLs. Alt text describes a claim about the image; it does not prove the pixels match.
2. Use an actual supported import, visible permitted download, connected browser handoff, or user upload to bring the product image/video into Lumina Canvas.
3. Open and inspect the real imported asset. Record its handle, source, view, variant, and visible details. Only then may it drive product-lock assets or image-conditioned generation.
4. If hotlinking, authentication, robots rules, or the native environment prevents import, try an official manual/media kit or another authorized source. Do not bypass access controls.
5. If no inspectable image is available, continue with a text-only evidence sheet and an explicitly provisional storyboard. Ask once for a product upload only when accurate appearance is necessary for production.

Never say “the product is red,” “the logo sits on the lid,” or “the demo shows X” because a filename, alt attribute, or snippet suggests it. Never call a generated image a scraped, downloaded, or inspected product photo.

## White-model recovery

A white model is a neutral intermediate representation for locking form and continuity. Use it when actual references exist but production needs cleaner views, or when verified dimensions/mechanics need to be staged before applying finishes.

Create a sheet with a hero three-quarter view plus front, side, back, top/bottom or open/closed views as relevant. Use neutral lighting and background, consistent scale, and no decorative text. For wearable products, include neutral mannequin/body-fit views without inventing anatomy-dependent performance. For products with controls, ports, seams, closures, or moving parts, show only verified elements.

Classify each visible property:

- **Locked:** directly visible across supplied/imported references or confirmed by the user.
- **Derived:** reasonable geometric interpolation needed to connect visible views; keep it conservative and disclose it.
- **Unknown:** hidden face, exact finish, unverified control, packaging copy, or internal mechanism.

Verified dimensions alone may support a dimensioned blockout, not a brand-faithful product. A generic blockout can guide composition, shadow, hand position, and camera distance, but must not appear as the final product in a truthful ad. Do not add logos, labels, certification marks, displays, ports, buttons, ingredients, or claims to make the model look finished.

## Product use-state evidence

For each planned product action record source/asset ID, inspected timecode or image region, visible state, permitted depiction, and unknown transition. A closed packshot supports a closed-package shot, not a lid-opening mechanism, cream/paste consistency, liquid flow, absorption, or efficacy. Do not infer exact texture or color from neutral white-model renders. Requested materials/colors may guide an explicitly proposed finish but are not verified product appearance until supported.

When the desired demonstration lacks evidence, keep the product in its observed state, use another supported feature, or ask one targeted question for the missing reference. Never use a generated visual as independent proof of the behavior it invented.

## Review and competitor research

Use reviews to identify language, objections, and scenarios, not to fabricate proof. Preserve the meaning of a quoted review, avoid composite testimonials presented as one person, and record whether the reviewer or purchase was verified when the platform exposes it. Do not invent a reviewer avatar, name, rating, or before/after result.

Competitor research may reveal category conventions and whitespace. Do not copy a competitor's exact script, trade dress, character, slogan, footage, or distinctive composition. Comparative claims require current, like-for-like evidence and jurisdiction-appropriate wording; otherwise convert the insight into a non-comparative product demonstration.

## Final landing-page reconciliation

Immediately before final review, recheck the landing page or current approved offer. Compare model/variant, visible product, price/discount, bundle contents, shipping/return language, CTA destination, disclaimers, and promotion dates against the edit. If a volatile field changed, patch only affected titles, voice, captions, CTA, and manifest, then review the new revision.
