## Directing rules derived from the reference

Keep the rhythm contour: **detail tease → hero/name reveal → repeated comic demonstration modules → emotional release**. The joke is delivered through a serious presentation of an ordinary or absurd action; avoid turning every beat into shouted dialogue, random memes, or transition effects.

- Opening detail shots: start by budgeting roughly **0.8–1.6s each**, followed by a **2–3.5s** hero hold and a short naming/reset beat when useful.
- Demonstration modules: budget roughly **4–7s each** for trigger, action and result. A larger escalation may earn **8–9s**, as the tissue sequence does. Within a module, use shorter **1–2.5s** inserts and longer result holds where needed.
- Reaction/payoff breathing room: initially allow **0.4–0.8s after the result becomes legible**, adjusting for actual performance. Do not cut before the viewer understands the consequence.
- Emotional finish: plan two or three calmer shots, roughly **3–4s each** in a 45–60s film. Allow the final gesture to complete.

These bands are adaptation starting points, not measured averages or hard model parameters. Judge duration from real action and speech. Use mostly motivated direct cuts; retain a deliberate fade/reset around the opening reveal if chosen. Do not add a flash, zoom, shake, transition, or subtitle change on every beat merely to look fast.
