# Advertising compliance, truthfulness, and final QC

Read this before creative approval, generation, final review, and export. This is a production safeguard, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice. When the category or market requires specialized review, preserve the draft and route it for qualified approval.

## Claim map

Every objective product claim in dialogue, captions, titles, visual demonstrations, packaging closeups, thumbnails, and CTA needs an evidence source and qualifier. Keep a canvas claim map:

| Claim ID | Exact wording/visual implication | Evidence | Scope/qualifier | Placement | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Check the net impression, not only literal words. A scene can imply “instant,” “guaranteed,” “waterproof,” “doctor approved,” “free,” or “limited” without saying it. Comic exaggeration is not automatically harmless when a reasonable viewer could treat it as a product result.

Do not invent or overstate:

- health, medical, cosmetic, weight, safety, financial, environmental, durability, performance, or comparative outcomes;
- certifications, awards, lab tests, expert approvals, ingredients, materials, compatibility, origin, or warranty;
- ratings, review counts, testimonials, creator usage, purchase verification, stock, sales volume, or popularity;
- price savings, free shipping, bundle contents, coupon eligibility, scarcity, countdowns, or promotion dates;
- before/after results, measured speed, battery life, capacity, size, fit, or “works for everyone.”

If evidence is missing, narrow the wording to a demonstrable feature, label the content as a fictional gag where appropriate, or remove it. Do not hide a material qualifier in unreadable text.

## Endorsements, people, and intellectual property

- Disclose material relationships and gifted/paid creator content where required.
- Do not fabricate a real customer, fake documentary interview, expert credential, or verified-purchase testimonial.
- Use supplied likeness, voice, logo, music, footage, and product media only within the authorized scope.
- Avoid public-figure impersonation and unlicensed creator/style cloning. Use category-level inspiration and original production content.
- Preserve trademarks accurately when showing the user's product. Do not imply competitor affiliation or copy distinctive competitor trade dress.

## Platform and audience safety

Honor the actual destination platform's current rules when known; check them at production time rather than relying on stale memory. Treat regulated goods, age-restricted products, health/beauty claims, finance, gambling, alcohol, political content, and content directed at children as high-risk categories requiring explicit policy review.

Do not create dangerous demonstrations, unsafe product use, fake emergency UI, malicious QR codes, deceptive system notifications, or instructions viewers might imitate harmfully. A funny plot must not obscure safe operation or required warnings.

## Pre-production gate

Before Ad Production Review confirm:

- product model/variant and visual references are real and current;
- every objective line and visual implication has a claim-map status;
- price/offer and CTA destination match the target market;
- disclosure and safety copy are included in the shot table with readable timing;
- rights/consent are recorded for product media, people, voice, music, and reference inputs;
- high-risk category review is complete or explicitly pending.

An unresolved material claim or rights problem blocks only dependent shots; keep independent draft work moving.

## Final editorial QC

Watch the complete current revision with sound and inspect it muted at phone size.

### Truth and product

- Correct SKU/variant, color, shape, logo/label, packaging, included parts, and use state.
- Demonstration and result match the evidence and landing page.
- No generated artifact changes a claim, safety feature, product scale, or mechanism.
- Price, discount math, coupon, dates, shipping, returns, and CTA are current.

### Story and conversion

- First frame communicates a specific tension or result; product role becomes clear quickly.
- Joke setup, escalation, product action, proof, payoff, and CTA are understandable.
- Humor supports the selling idea and does not belittle the customer or distract from proof.
- Hook variants change the declared variable and hand off cleanly to the shared body.

### Picture, text, and sound

- Correct duration, ratio, crop, platform safe areas, and no black gaps/duplicate spans.
- Text is exact, readable, sufficiently long, correctly layered, and not unintentionally generated into the scene.
- Dialogue is complete and intelligible; lip sync is acceptable where needed.
- Effects and ambience support the action; music is authorized, balanced, and does not cover speech.
- Captions match final speech and cuts; disclosures remain visible and legible.

### Delivery

- Final approval matches the exact editor revision exported.
- Each file opens, plays, and has expected picture/audio/caption state.
- Variant filenames and manifest map to the correct hook/body/payoff/CTA modules.
- Download access works; editable draft and supporting artifacts are returned when available.

Publishing, campaign creation, budget allocation, audience targeting, or claims of measured performance are outside video-production approval and require separate authorization and real campaign data.
